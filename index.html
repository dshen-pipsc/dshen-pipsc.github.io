Dear fellow IT members,

I am seeking your support to serve as Vice-President of the IT Group National Executive in the 2027 election.

Throughout my career, I have always believed in making a positive difference, whether through helping a colleague, supporting a member through a difficult situation, or working with others to find solutions to complex challenges. I bring this same passion, dedication, and sense of responsibility to my union work.

Union Experience

A decade of union involvement

Over the past decade, I have had the privilege of serving our members in various capacities:

  • Active Steward2015–Present
  • Executive, IT Ottawa West Subgroup2016–Present
  • Treasurer, IT Ottawa West Subgroup2016
  • Communication Officer, IT Ottawa West Subgroup2017
  • President, IT Ottawa West Subgroup2019–Present
  • Member-at-Large, Ottawa West Branch2022–2025
  • Communication Lead, ESDC Consultation Team2024–Present

These roles have taught me that effective leadership is not about having all the answers. It is about being approachable, understanding different perspectives, making informed decisions, and being accountable for the outcomes.

As President of one of the IT Group's largest subgroups, I have worked through organizational changes, different opinions, and challenging situations. I have learned the importance of staying open-minded, building trust, and finding common ground without compromising the interests of the members we represent.

Vision & Priorities

My Approach to Leadership

01 Members First — Listen, Understand, and Take Action

Every member's concern deserves attention, regardless of their position, background, or circumstances.

I believe listening is only the first step. Members need timely advice, meaningful support, and representatives who are prepared to follow through. When I cannot resolve an issue directly, I work to identify the right resources, connect with the right people, and pursue a practical way forward.

I will continue to advocate for fair representation, responsible use of member dues, and decisions that reflect the interests of our membership.

02 Collaboration — Respect Different Perspectives, Achieve Results Together

Our strength as a union comes from our members and the diverse experiences, knowledge, and ideas they bring.

I welcome constructive criticism and believe that disagreements, when handled respectfully, can lead to better decisions. Being open-minded does not mean avoiding difficult decisions. It means considering different perspectives, evaluating the facts, and being willing to adjust our approach when a better solution emerges.

I believe in recognizing people's strengths, encouraging participation, and building productive relationships. We may not always agree, but we should always be able to work together with mutual respect and a shared purpose.

03 Engagement — Stay Connected, Informed, and Involved

The IT profession is evolving rapidly. Artificial intelligence, changing workplace expectations, remote and hybrid work arrangements, and workforce pressures are reshaping our professional environment.

Our union must be equally prepared to adapt.

I want to encourage greater member participation, improve communication, and create more opportunities for members to share their ideas and concerns.

I believe transparency builds trust. Members deserve to understand what their representatives are working on, why decisions are made, and what progress has been achieved.

Engagement should be an ongoing conversation, not something that happens only during elections.

If Elected

My Priorities as Vice-President

If elected, I will focus my efforts on areas that directly affect our members:

Fair and Effective Collective Bargaining

Advocate for competitive compensation, fair working conditions, and collective agreements that reflect the evolving responsibilities and contributions of IT professionals.

Training and Professional Development

Support responsible oversight and effective use of the TBS co-managed IT Training and Development Fund, ensuring members have meaningful opportunities to develop their skills and prepare for emerging technologies.

Workforce Adjustment and Return to Office

Advocate for transparent, fair, and evidence-based approaches that protect members' rights, recognize operational realities, and support a healthy work-life balance.

Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion

Promote fair treatment, equal opportunities, and meaningful action to address systemic discrimination and barriers affecting visible minorities and other underrepresented members.

Across all these priorities, I will emphasize responsible decision-making, transparency, accountability, and measurable progress.

Moving Forward, Together

I know that no single individual has all the solutions. Meaningful progress requires experience, fresh perspectives, honest conversations, and a willingness to work together.

I am proud of what I have contributed over the past decade, but I also recognize that there is always room to learn, improve, and do better.

I am seeking this position not simply to represent members, but to help build a stronger, more engaged, and more responsive IT Group — one where members feel valued, supported, and confident that their voices matter.

My commitment is simple: Listen with an open mind. Act with purpose. Lead with fairness and respect. Deliver with accountability.

Together, we can build on our strengths, address our challenges, and create a more positive future for all IT members.

I would be honoured to earn your trust and support.

Vote for Experience. Vote for Engagement. Vote for Action.
